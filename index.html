Smilegate takes over Lost Ark West

Early 2027 · Specific date TBA

37days until January 1, 2027

Countdown to the start of 2027 (UTC), not a confirmed takeover date. The actual transition countdown will be available once an exact date is announced.

Amazon Games will hand Western live-service operations back to Smilegate. Lost Ark is not shutting down. Smilegate aims to gradually narrow the update gap with Korea.

What Western players need to know
  • Steam, characters, inventory, progression, entitlements and wallet balances carry over for players who did not opt out.
  • First login after the handover will require linking Steam to Smilegate and accepting its Terms of Service.
  • Claim wanted mail attachments before maintenance; mail text and chat history are not transferred.
  • According to Lost Ark Support, the opt-out window closed on November 10, 2026.
