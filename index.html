What changes?

Smilegate takes over from Amazon Games. The game continues, with plans to gradually close the KR–West update gap.

  • Steam accounts, characters, inventory, progression, entitlements and wallet balances carry over for players who did not opt out.
  • First login requires linking Steam and accepting Smilegate's Terms of Service.
  • Claim wanted mail attachments before maintenance; mail text and chat history do not transfer.
  • Lost Ark Support lists November 10, 2026 as the opt-out deadline.
